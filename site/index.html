FROM 2021 → 2026

An old project, rebuilt as a living system.

Sleep Predictor began as a data-science portfolio project. The original notebook explored multiple regression approaches and reduced the product experience to five practical inputs. This public version turns that work into a self-contained browser experience.

01

Load the data

The site loads the project’s original semicolon-delimited sleep dataset directly from the deployed GitHub Pages bundle.

02

Engineer the signals

Sleep quality becomes the target. Coffee and workout signals are extracted from sleep notes; zero step values are treated as missing, matching the modern Python rebuild.

03

Train in the browser

A Ridge regression model standardizes the five features, imputes missing values with medians, and fits directly in JavaScript.

04

Predict privately

Your inputs stay on your device. The trained model produces an exploratory score between 0 and 100 without calling a backend.

ORIGINAL MODELING EVIDENCE

The first notebook already contained a real modeling story.

The original 2021 notebook recorded a mean-prediction baseline MAE of 10.56 and compared Linear Regression, Ridge, Random Forest, and XGBoost. Ridge recorded the lowest three-fold cross-validation MAE among those preserved notebook runs at approximately 8.29.

Historical approachRecorded MAE
Mean baseline10.56
